# Lanternwake 3D: Story Bible

Status: **draft v3**. Direction "Relight the Beacons", silent hero named Wren,
XP levels plus workbench gear, cold-open hook, ember boss intros, choice
ending (Vesk joins the village if spared), revisitable areas, cold open skippable after first clear. Approved by the user on 2026-11-22. The open
questions are at the bottom.

Everything here is original. The genre is a top-down/third-person action
adventure with dungeons, and nothing is borrowed from any existing game: no
names, items, layouts or music.

---

## 1. Pitch

**Premise.** Cinderhollow is a lantern-keepers' village on the edge of the
fog-mire. Five beacons once held the fog back: four on the hills around the
village, and a mother-flame in the shrine at the centre of the mire. One by
one the hill beacons have gone dark. On the night the last one dies, the fog
pours in and takes old Keeper Oda. Her apprentice, Wren, is the only keeper
left.

**Hero.** Wren, apprentice lantern-keeper, about 16. Silent. Players read Wren
as stubborn and kind through what they do: Wren helps everyone and always goes
back for a lost lantern.

**Villain.** Vesk the Wickthief. He was Oda's first apprentice and was exiled
for trying to keep a beacon's flame for himself. Light keeps him alive, so he
has been draining the beacons. He has bound the Mirewarden, a great mire
spirit, to guard the shrine while he drinks the mother-flame.

**Tone.** Warm and classic: bright moments in dark places. The villagers are
funny and the world is eerie. Nobody gives speeches, and every line is short.
Each lit beacon visibly pushes the fog back from the hub map, so the player
can see their progress.

**Retro feel.** Low-poly, chunky pixel textures, thick fog and lantern-lit
darkness. The lantern is the main light source, so its radius is part of the
gameplay.

## 3. Plot in Acts

### Cold Open: "You Lit Them All" (about 2 min), the hook
The game starts at the *end*.
1. No title screen yet. The game fades in on the Drowned Shrine's mother-flame chamber. Wren has all four lantern modes, full hearts and the top gear.
2. Vesk's boss intro plays, then a short **playable** fight. It is tuned so the player can't win: after about 60 s, or once Wren loses 3 hearts, Vesk steals the lantern's flame.
3. Wren falls. Vesk: *"You lit them all for me. Every last one."* Oda calls Wren's name from above. The screen goes black.
4. Title card: **LANTERNWAKE**. Then: *"Three nights earlier…"* and the prologue starts at dusk in Cinderhollow.

Why: before the slower gathering tutorial, players have already seen the villain, all four powers and the goal, along with a mystery (why does lighting the beacons help him?).

**Payoff:** the real finale replays this exact moment (see the Vesk fight in 6.5). This time, when Wren falls, the Mirewarden that Wren freed rises and shields Wren, and the fight carries on into phase 3. The cold open was a real moment, and the player now knows what happens next.

### Prologue: The Last Beacon (hub, about 7 min)
This is the current 2D loop, rebuilt as the tutorial.
1. Dusk. Oda sends Wren to gather 3 timber and 2 ironstone (teaches movement, attack and gathering).
2. Wren forges the **Ember Lantern** at Bram's workbench (teaches the lantern and oil).
3. The last beacon on Thornwood hill goes out, and fog floods the gate. Three shades attack (teaches dodge roll and lock-on).
4. Cutscene: a tall figure in the fog (Vesk, unnamed for now) lifts Oda's lantern and the fog swallows them both. Oda's **Beacon Map** drops at Wren's feet.

### Act I: Kindling (Thornwood, then Quarry)
- **Thornwood.** Wren burns a path through the brambles, defeats Gnarlback and lights the first beacon. The fog pulls back from the north road. A voice in the smoke says: *"One candle. How brave."*
- **Rattlecut Quarry.** Wren gets the **Glimmer Lens**, defeats Slagmother and lights the second beacon. At the top, Vesk appears in person, and at this point he only steals *half* of the beacon's flame before vanishing.
- **Midpoint (back in the hub).** Bram and Tansy recognise the description. Bram explains who Vesk is: Oda's first apprentice, exiled. Oda left Wren a sealed letter "for when he comes back" (it's read at the end).

### Act II: The Deep Fog (Fen, then Cliffs)
- **Saltreed Fen.** Wren gets the **Moonwick**, which reveals ghost paths and false lights. Old Nell says the beacons don't *destroy* the fog, they *push* it, and all of it is being pushed toward the shrine. Wren defeats the Stilt Widow and lights the third beacon.
- **Galeback Cliffs.** Wren gets the **Bellows Lantern**, defeats Kitesnatcher and lights the fourth beacon. From the cliff top Wren can see the whole mire draining toward the Drowned Shrine, where a huge light is being drunk.
- **Twist.** Vesk wanted Wren to light the beacons. A fully pushed fog pins the Mirewarden in the shrine, and the mother-flame is easier to drink when it's all he has to fight for. Oda is inside, chained in light as his fuel.

### Act III: The Drowned Shrine (finale)
- With all four beacons lit, the mire drains enough to reveal the causeway. The final dungeon uses every power.
- **Mirewarden.** Wren defeats it, and as it falls it lets go of the chains of light. It isn't evil, only bound.
- **Vesk.** He fights with stolen versions of Wren's own powers. When beaten, the stolen light pours out of him back to the beacons, and he shrinks to an old man with a guttering wick.
- **Ending.** Oda is freed and reads her letter aloud: she always hoped Vesk would come home. Wren chooses whether to **light Vesk's wick** (mercy ending: Vesk becomes the shrine's keeper) or **leave it dark** (he fades into the fog). Both endings show dawn. Wren is named Keeper of Cinderhollow.

## 4. Core Mechanics (design view)

- **Sword:** combo of up to 3 swings; a charged **Ember Strike** once the Ember Lantern is forged.
- **Dodge roll** with i-frames (kept from 2D).
- **Lock-on** to the nearest foe (needed for 3D boss fights).
- **Hearts:** start with 3 hearts, max 10. The pickup that drops from shades heals 1 heart.
- **Lantern oil:** a meter. Every lantern power costs oil, and it refills from oil jars, glowmoths and the beacons. The lantern's light radius shrinks a little as oil runs low (atmosphere plus a hint).
- **Lantern modes:** one lantern, four modes, swapped with a key or the d-pad. Each is gained in a different area.
- **Checkpoints:** lit braziers in each area act as checkpoints (kept from 2D: full heal, foes reset, progress kept).
- **Fog:** each lit beacon thins the hub fog and reveals a little more of the overworld.

## 5. Hub: Cinderhollow

**Mood.** A cosy village under heavy fog. It has a well, two cottages, Bram's
forge and the beacon map on the village board. Night always, but the fog lifts
step by step.

**Exits** (each locked by the previous area's tool):
| Exit | Leads to | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| North road | Thornwood | Open after the prologue |
| East cut | Rattlecut Quarry | Thick brambles: burn them with the **Ember Lantern** |
| South dyke | Saltreed Fen | Sealed sluice door with a sun-crystal lock: hit it with a **Glimmer Lens** beam |
| West chasm | Galeback Cliffs | No bridge: the **Moonwick** reveals a ghost bridge |
| Mire causeway | Drowned Shrine | Underwater until **all 4 beacons** are lit, then the **Bellows** clears the fog wall |

**Hub services:** Bram's workbench (upgrades), the beacon map (shows
progress), Tansy (hints, and a glowmoth side quest), a well (refills oil).

**Hub puzzle (optional, teaches pushing):** push the crate from Tansy's yard
onto the cellar hatch plate to open the cellar, which holds a Heart Ember.

## 6. Areas

### 6.1 Thornwood (dusk forest)
- **Mood/biome:** a dense dusk forest with orange leaves, root bridges, fireflies and thick bramble walls.
- **Tool gained:** **Ember Lantern** (forged in the prologue, mastered here). Modes: light braziers and burn brambles/webs. The Ember Strike is unlocked here.
- **Puzzles:**
  1. *Brazier gate:* light 3 braziers within 10 seconds to open a root gate (they burn out).
  2. *Burn the bridge rope:* burn a rope so a log drops across a stream and becomes a bridge (physics: a hinged log).
  3. *Rolling seed-pod:* push a giant seed-pod down a slope so it smashes a rotten wall (rolling boulder).
  4. *Bramble lift:* burn the brambles holding down a counterweight so a lift platform rises.
- **Enemies:** Shade (basic lunge), Burrling (a seed ball that rolls at you; burn it to pop it), Thornbat (a flier that only dives inside your light radius).
- **Boss: Gnarlback**, a giant boar armoured in roots.
  - *Arena:* a clearing ringed by 4 thick tree-pillars and 2 braziers.
  - *Pattern:* paws the ground for 1 s, then charges in a straight line; turns slowly. Phase 2 (below 50%): root spikes erupt in a line toward Wren after each charge, and it charges twice in a row.
  - *Weak point:* bait it into a tree-pillar so it stuns itself (3 s). Burn the brambles on its back with the Ember Lantern to expose the soft hide, then slash. Sword hits on its armoured front bounce off.
- **Reward:** Thornwood beacon lit, Heart Ember, +XP, Root Hide (an upgrade material).

### 6.2 Rattlecut Quarry (mines and old machines)
- **Mood/biome:** terraced pits, mine tunnels, minecart rails, lava cracks, rusty cranes. Warm red light in the deep, cold blue up top.
- **Tool gained:** **Glimmer Lens**, a focused beam. It bounces off mirrors, powers sun-crystals and stuns some foes.
- **Puzzles:**
  1. *Mirror relay:* rotate 3 mirror stands so the beam travels from the lantern to a crystal door.
  2. *Minecart smash:* push a loaded cart along rails; switch the track lever so it rams a boulder wall.
  3. *Counterweight crane:* load ore sacks onto a crane bucket until it lowers a platform (weight plate).
  4. *Lava cooling:* beam a hanging ice-crystal so it melts and drips, cooling lava into a walkable crust for 8 s.
- **Enemies:** Shade, Cinderbat (sets you on fire; roll to put it out), Rockjaw (shelled; beam it to make it flip, then hit its belly), Slagling (a slow lava slug that leaves hot trails).
- **Boss: Slagmother**, a molten golem in the old smelter.
  - *Arena:* a round smelter floor with a minecart rail loop around the edge and 3 wall mirrors.
  - *Pattern:* slow walk; a ground slam that leaves lava pools (dodge them); in phase 2 she throws slag balls that roll across the floor.
  - *Weak point:* her core is hidden by cooled slag plates. Ram her with a minecart (lever on the rail) to knock a plate off, then bounce the Lens beam off a wall mirror into the exposed core. She has 3 plates, so this is a 3-cycle fight.
- **Reward:** Quarry beacon lit (half stolen by Vesk in a cutscene), Heart Ember, Slag Iron (material).

### 6.3 Saltreed Fen (misty marsh)
- **Mood/biome:** flooded reed beds, stilt houses, sunken lighthouse, false lights, the thickest fog in the game. Sounds of frogs and water.
- **Tool gained:** **Moonwick**, a pale glow. Inside its radius it reveals ghost bridges, hidden doors and true enemy positions, and it unmasks fake wisp-lights.
- **Puzzles:**
  1. *Ghost stepping stones:* cross a lake on stones that are only solid while lit by the Moonwick (oil drains while you hold it).
  2. *Floating crates:* push crates into the water to make a raft path; they drift with a current, so time your jumps.
  3. *Two-plate lock:* stand on one pressure plate and push a waterlogged crate onto the other to open the sluice.
  4. *Lighthouse lamp:* climb the sunken lighthouse, relight its lamp and aim it (rotating) to reveal a huge ghost path across the fen.
- **Enemies:** Shade, Mudling (rises out of the mud and grabs; roll free), Wisp-lure (a fake light that leads you into pits; Moonwick reveals it), Bog Shade (only visible in Moonwick light).
- **Boss: The Stilt Widow**, a tall heron-witch on stilt legs.
  - *Arena:* a ring of wooden platforms over deep mud; fog everywhere.
  - *Pattern:* she makes 3 fog copies of herself. A beak stab from above, telegraphed by a shadow; a sweeping reed-scythe across a platform; summons 2 Mudlings. In phase 2 the platforms start sinking and ghost bridges appear between them (Moonwick).
  - *Weak point:* only the real one casts a shadow in Moonwick light. When she stabs, her beak sticks in the planks for 2 s, and that's the moment to hit her head.
- **Reward:** Fen beacon lit, Heart Ember, Heron Quill (material).

### 6.4 Galeback Cliffs (wind, windmills, rope bridges)
- **Mood/biome:** high sea cliffs, windmills, rope bridges, crashing waves far below, strong gusts. The fog is thinner here and the stars show.
- **Tool gained:** **Bellows Lantern**, a gust of hot air. It pushes crates and enemies, spins fan-wheels, blows out flames and clears fog walls.
- **Puzzles:**
  1. *Windmill lift:* blow on a windmill's sails to raise a basket lift (spin a fan, which drives a winch).
  2. *Rope bridge sway:* gusts swing the bridge, so cross between gusts, or blow the crate across to weigh it down and steady it.
  3. *Crate off the ledge:* blow a crate across a gap onto a pressure plate you can't reach.
  4. *Boulder run:* blow out the torches holding a gate, then roll a boulder down a chute to smash a wall below (rolling boulder plus timing).
- **Enemies:** Shade, Gustling (a small wind spirit that knocks you toward edges; Bellows pushes it back), Cliff Shrike (a diving bird), Crag Crab (a side-walker with a big claw; only hittable from behind).
- **Boss: Kitesnatcher**, a great sky-ray with glowing gill-lights.
  - *Arena:* the top of the tallest windmill: a round deck with 3 big fan-wheels and no railings.
  - *Pattern:* circles the deck, then swoops in a line (dodge). Hovers to charge a gust-bomb that pushes Wren toward the edge. In phase 2 it swoops twice and tail-whips the deck.
  - *Weak point:* while it hovers, blow on the 3 fan-wheels with the Bellows to make an updraft that flips it onto the deck. While it's grounded, hit its gill-lights.
- **Reward:** Cliffs beacon lit, Heart Ember, Sky Hide (material).

### 6.5 The Drowned Shrine (finale)
- **Mood/biome:** a sunken temple of keepers. Water drained to knee height, ancient lantern statues, green light, Oda's light glowing from deep inside.
- **Tool gained:** none. It's the exam: every mode is needed. The reward is a **Keeper's Crown** cosmetic plus the ending.
- **Puzzles:**
  1. *Four statues:* each statue asks for one mode (burn, beam, reveal, blow) to open the inner door.
  2. *Drain-wheel:* blow on a water wheel to lower the water so a sunken path appears, then push a block onto it.
  3. *Light chain:* beam through mirrors while the Moonwick reveals which mirrors are real.
  4. *Weight hall:* push 3 heavy keeper-statues onto plates; the last needs the Bellows to slide it across ice-slick stone.
- **Enemies:** Drowned Keeper (an armoured shade with a lantern-shield; burn the shield first), plus a mix of the earlier foes.
- **Boss 1: The Mirewarden** (kept from 2D, extended).
  - *Pattern:* phase 1 as in 2D: slow advance, red pulse every 4 s (roll through it), sags for 1.5 s after the pulse (hit it). Phase 2: it sinks into the mire and rises under Wren; the Moonwick shows the ripple where it will rise.
  - *Weak point:* hittable while dazed after a pulse; only ember light (Ember Strike or lantern-lit blade) wounds it.
- **Boss 2: Vesk the Wickthief.**
  - *Arena:* the mother-flame chamber; Oda is held in light above the centre.
  - *Pattern:* quick human-sized duellist. Phase 1: sword combos, blocks, steals one of Wren's modes (greyed out for 15 s). Phase 2: throws stolen flames that copy the earlier bosses (a Gnarlback-style charge, a Kitesnatcher-style gust). At the end of phase 2, **the cold-open moment replays**: Vesk snuffs the lantern and Wren falls. The freed Mirewarden rises and shields Wren, re-lights the lantern, and phase 3 begins. Phase 3: a shade cloak makes him invisible outside Moonwick light.
  - *Weak point:* the stolen-light lantern on his back. Blow his cloak away with the Bellows, then beam the lantern with the Glimmer Lens to crack it; finish with an Ember Strike.
  - *Cold-open version:* phases 1-2 only, with damage tuned up. It ends on a scripted snuff once 60 s pass or Wren loses 3 hearts. The player can't lose a life here. **It can't be skipped on the first playthrough.** Once any save has finished the game, a "Skip" prompt appears.

### 6.6 Boss Intros
Every boss gets a short, punchy intro (6-10 s, skippable on a retry). One shared format, with a different beat for each boss:
1. **Arena lock:** the doors slam shut, the fog rolls in and the music cuts.
2. **Reveal:** the camera sweeps to the boss as it does its signature move.
3. **Name card:** the name **burns in letter by letter like embers** across the lower third, with the subtitle under it, then blows away as ash. This look is Lanternwake's own: orange embers on black.
4. **Roar/beat:** the camera snaps back behind Wren and the boss music starts.

| Boss | Reveal beat | Card |
|---|---|---|
| Gnarlback | A "hill" in the clearing stands up, shakes off its roots and paws the ground. | GNARLBACK, Root of the Wood |
| Slagmother | The smelter vat tips over. The molten pour stands up into a body and the plates cool with a hiss. | SLAGMOTHER, Heart of the Smelter |
| Stilt Widow | Three heron silhouettes stalk out of the fog in perfect sync, then all turn their heads at once. | THE STILT WIDOW, She Who Waits in Reeds |
| Kitesnatcher | Wings blot out the stars. It swoops past the windmill, the sails splinter and it circles. | KITESNATCHER, Ray of the High Wind |
| Mirewarden | The mire floor rises into a giant hung with old drowned lanterns; chains of light hold its wrists. | THE MIREWARDEN, Bound Guardian |
| Vesk | Vesk drinks a sip of flame from Oda's lantern, smiles and draws a blade of stolen light. | VESK, the Wickthief |

### 6.7 Revisiting Areas
Every area stays open after its boss. Its beacon stays lit, the fog there is
thin, the foes respawn when you re-enter, and any puzzle you solved stays
solved. You come back to find missed Heart Embers and glowmoths, often behind a
power you only got later (for example, a Moonwick ghost path in Thornwood). The
beacon top can be used for fast travel back to the hub. Boss arenas stay empty
(a boss rematch is stretch).

### Collectibles
- **Heart Embers:** collect 4 to make 1 heart. There are 8 in the game (one per area boss, one in the hub cellar, three hidden behind puzzle side-rooms), so they add 2 hearts in total.
- **Oil jars:** break them to refill oil.
- **Glowmoths:** 20 hidden around the world. Bring them to Tansy for Glow and, at 20, a stretch reward: a lantern colour swap.

### Lantern modes (gating order)
1. **Ember** (prologue/Thornwood): light braziers, burn brambles, Ember Strike.
2. **Glimmer Lens** (Quarry): beam, mirrors, crystals.
3. **Moonwick** (Fen): reveal ghost paths, hidden foes, fake lights.
4. **Bellows** (Cliffs): gust pushes objects and foes, spins fans, clears fog.

### After the Credits (post-game)
After the credits the game reloads into Cinderhollow **at dawn**, with no fog.
The player can keep revisiting areas to finish collectibles. Oda is home by
her cottage.

**Mercy ending:** Vesk lives in the village and sits by the well, mending
lanterns. His lines cycle:
- **Vesk:** Morning, Keeper. Strange word to say to you.
- **Vesk:** Bram lets me use the bench. Barely.
- **Vesk:** Every wick in this village, I've stolen from once.
- **Vesk:** Now I fix them. Seems fair.
- **Vesk (all Heart Embers found):** You never leave a light behind. She raised you right.
- **Bram:** He's slow at the bench. But careful. Good.
- **Tansy:** Vesk showed me how to catch glowmoths with no jar!
- **Oda:** Two apprentices again. Lord help me.

**Dark ending:** there's a cold, unlit lantern on a post by the well. Its
interact text is *"A lantern that was never relit."*
- **Oda:** Leave it there, sprout. Some lights you just remember.

## 10. Scope Notes (one coding agent)

- **Reuse one kit everywhere:** pushable crate, pressure plate, brazier
  (lit/unlit), mirror stand (rotatable), beam receiver crystal, rolling boulder,
  rope bridge, fan-wheel, ghost platform (visible/solid only in Moonwick),
  lever/track switch, door. Every puzzle above is a combination of these
  11 pieces.
- **Enemies:** 1 base AI (wander, aggro, wind-up, attack, recover; kept from
  2D shades) with small variations. The 14 named foes can share 5 base meshes
  with recolours.
- **Bosses:** each is one state machine with 2 phases and 1 weak-point rule.
  Vesk is the only 3-phase fight.
- **Areas:** each is one map with about 3 rooms plus a boss arena plus a
  beacon top. The hub is one open map.
- **Cutscenes:** in-engine, text boxes plus simple camera moves. No voice acting.
- **Must-have (user asked for these):** the cold open (skippable only after
  the first clear), boss intros with ember name cards, the mercy/dark ending
  choice, Vesk in the village after the mercy ending, and revisitable areas.
- **Post-game:** the same hub map with a dawn lighting preset, the fog turned
  off, and NPCs placed according to the `veskSpared` flag. Vesk reuses his
  boss model, unarmed, in a sitting pose.
- **Boss intros:** one reusable intro script (lock, camera path, name card,
  music cue). Each boss only adds its camera path and one animation.
- **Cold open:** reuses the final Vesk arena and fight with a "fullyPowered"
  save preset and a scripted snuff trigger. No new assets.
- **Stretch (cut first if time runs short):**
  - Boss rematches in revisited areas.
  - Vesk's phase 2 boss copies (just make it a faster phase 1).
  - The Stilt Widow's sinking platforms in phase 2.
  - The glowmoth side quest and lantern colour swap.
  - Minecart riding (pushing only is the base).
  - Thornbat "only dives in light" behaviour.
  - The Heart Ember side-rooms (keep only the boss and cellar ones).
